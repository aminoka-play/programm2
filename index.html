  1. Choose a route.
    • Check distance and duration.
    • Read the preparation checklist.

    • Comfortable shoes
    • Weather-appropriate clothing
  3. Complete the application form.
What does leave no trace mean?

Leave no trace means respecting nature by taking your trash with you, staying on trails, and minimizing your impact on the environment.
